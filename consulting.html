Consulting &
my dream

My dream is to help make other people's dreams real. My expertise lies in health, fitness, modeling, and visual storytelling. If you know what you want (or don't even know) make yourself proud. Interested? A first 30min consultation in any field is free and could be the foundation of changing your life.

Where can I help you the most, and why me?

Sustainability, experience, and understanding of people. These three pillars form the foundation of my expertise.

01 · Sustainability

I provide realistic assessments in sports, nutrition, and modeling. The focus is not on short-term solutions that promise rapid weight loss, temporary performance gains, or quick success in securing a large modeling job. The focus lies on building sustainable routines, developing honesty toward oneself, and strengthening personal integrity.

I hold people accountable for their actions while also offering a space where difficulties and challenges can be addressed openly. Many of these struggles are familiar from personal experience and remain part of ongoing development.

The aim is to provide practical tools to deal with self-doubt and fear, to clarify personal direction, and to pursue meaningful goals with consistency.

02 · Experience

I have eight years of experience in strength training and have been focusing specifically on longevity and micronutrient intake for the past three years. I first stood in front of a camera at the age of fourteen and have since worked with around seventy different photographers and creatives. My work includes fashion, skincare, insurance and financial services, retail campaigns, photo workshops, social media campaigns, and national television commercials.

I am represented by three agencies in Switzerland, Germany, and Italy and have been able to turn what was once a hobby into another professional and financially rewarding pillar.

03 · Understanding of people and their needs

Through my diverse activities in professional environments, modeling, numerous student and sports organizations, as well as a strong affinity for travel and social interaction, I have developed a well-trained understanding of people and the ability to guide and advise individuals from very different backgrounds.
